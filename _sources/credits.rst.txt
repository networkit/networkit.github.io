History
~~~~~~~

NetworKit is maintained by the research group `Exascale Algorithm Engineering <https://eae.iti.kit.edu/english>`_ of the Scientific Computing Center at the `Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) <https://www.kit.edu/english/>`_.
Until 2025, the `Research Group Modeling and Analysis of Complex Systems <https://www.informatik.hu-berlin.de/de/forschung/gebiete/macsy>`_ of the Institute of Computer Science at the `Humboldt-University of Berlin <https://www.hu-berlin.de/de>`_ maintained NetworKit.
NetworKit was seeded by the project `Parallel analysis of dynamic networks -- Algorithm engineering of efficient combinatorial and numerical methods <https://web.archive.org/web/20240706140018/http://parco.iti.kit.edu/forschung-en.shtml>`_, for which we acknowledge
financial support by MWK Baden-Württemberg. In this project Henning Meyerhenke was the principal investigator and Christian L. Staudt was the main PhD student. Since NetworKit's start in 2013, it has grown well
beyond the original MWK project and our group, with contributors and users from all over the world.

External Code
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The program source includes:

- the `tlx <https://github.com/tlx/tlx>`_ collection of C++ data structures, algorithms and helpers by Timo Bingmann and contributors
- the `TTMath <https://www.ttmath.org/>`_ bignum library by Tomasz Sowa
- the `Dynamic Hashed Blocks (DHB) <https://github.com/hu-macsy/dhb>`_ data structure for dynamic graphs by Alexander van der Grinten, Maria Predari and Florian Willich
- the `GoogleTest <https://github.com/google/googletest>`_ framework, which runs our unit tests
